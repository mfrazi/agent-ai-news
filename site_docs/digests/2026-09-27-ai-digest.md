# AI Intelligence Digest — Past 7 Days
**Coverage:** Frontier LLMs · Reasoning Models · Open Weights · Multimodal
**Window:** ~September 17–24, 2026 (with adjacent releases noted)

## Executive Summary

- **Frontier pricing war erupted on a single day.** On Sept 22, OpenAI (GPT-6 Sol/Luna) and Anthropic (Claude Opus 5.5) both cut API prices sharply — OpenAI ~50% below prior rates, Anthropic ~20% below Opus 5 — reframing frontier competition around **cost-per-completed-task** rather than raw benchmark scores.
- **1M-token context is now table stakes.** GPT-6 Astra/Sol/Luna (1.05M), Claude Opus 5.5 (1M), Gemini 3.8 Flash (1M), and DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (1M) all ship million-token windows, with reasoning-effort levels exposed as a standard API knob.
- **The open-weight frontier is increasingly Chinese and increasingly custom-licensed.** DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, and Kimi now ship the models most teams self-host, but licenses are drifting from permissive (Apache/MIT) toward bespoke terms (Qwen3.8-Max, Kimi K3).
- **Multimodal convergence hit real-time.** Gemini 3.8 Live Avatar (lip-synced video personas, 97 languages), OpenAI GPT-Live-1 (full-duplex voice), and native-audio video generation (FLUX 3, LTX-2.5) all landed within the window.
- **Research focus is shifting from capability to elicitation and control.** Notable papers argue RL for reasoning is largely *sparse policy selection* rather than capability learning, while test-time scaling, verifiers, and world models dominate the frontier.

## 2. Academic & Frontier Research

### Frontier LLMs (Architecture, Scaling, Efficiency)
- **Self-Play Pretraining with Zero Data** — arXiv:2609.30063. Self-play pretraining from random init with *no natural data*; generator proposes programs for a universal Turing machine, learner trains on resulting byte sequences. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30063)
- **How Model Growth, Recursion, and Boundary Operators Influence Scaling Exponents** — Shows architectural interventions can modify compute-optimal *scaling exponents*, not just constants; looped transformers with depth growth + normalize-and-reinject boundary operator improve loss scaling. [Walkthrough](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sNju_3z_wrU)
- **Time-Incremental Continued Pretraining of LLMs** — arXiv:2609.23916. Continues pretraining six open-weight models (OLMO2, Llama-3.x, Gemma-3) to study forgetting vs. knowledge acquisition. [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.23916)
- **Neural Spectral Capacity** — HF 2609.23087. Structural capacity metric beyond #Params/#FLOPs; frames Transformer design as capacity allocation. [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.23087)
- **SpectralShift** — arXiv:2609.14320. Context-window extension of Gated DeltaNet via spectral reparameterization. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14320)
- **Your Transformer Can Hold Two Thoughts at Once** — HF 2609.29845 (66 upvotes). Demonstrates linear superposition: linearly combined inputs yield superposed outputs. [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29845)
- **Flattening Every Memory Peak in Long-Context MoE Training** — arXiv:2609.14306. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.14306)

### Reasoning Models (Test-Time Compute, RL, Verifiers)
- **Planned Test-Time Scaling with Coordinated Reasoning Paths** — arXiv:2609.27374 (Xueqing Wu et al.). Plans/coordinates multiple reasoning paths at test time. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.27374)
- **Scaling of Capability and Efficiency at Inference Time in Large Reasoning Models** — arXiv:2609.27166. Studies overthinking and inverse-scaling phenomena. [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.27166)
- **Agentic Verifier** — arXiv:2604.16004. Multi-turn, tool-augmented reward modeling with forward/backward agents; 4B variant reportedly beats SOTA open reward models by ~25.2%. [alphaXiv](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2604.16004)
- **When and Where to Trust the Teacher** — arXiv:2609.28385. Unifies on-policy distillation and GRPO via entropy-calibrated credit assignment. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28385)
- **Rethinking RL for LLM Reasoning: It's Sparse Policy Selection, Not Capability Learning** — Position paper arguing RL mainly selects policies rather than expanding capability. [DailyArXiv](https://github.com/NeoFii/DailyArXiv/issues/163)
- **Capable yet Parsimonious: Extracting Hidden Chain-of-Thought in Frontier Models** — HF 2609.26637 (15 upvotes). Probes hidden CoT in closed models. [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.26637)
- **Agent-Editing World Model** — HF 2609.28416 (17 upvotes). Models only high-entropy tool results for efficient long-horizon planning. [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.28416)
- **Self-Organizing Agent Teams Learn to Reason Together** — HF 2609.22682. [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.22682)

### Open Weights (Recipes, Datasets, Reproducibility)
- **Rufus-Air: An Open LLM Post-Training Recipe** — arXiv:2609.29421 (Amazon; Chia-Yuan Chang et al.). Eight-stage pipeline on GLM-4.5-Air-Base (106B-A12B); key finding that SFT is capability-building, not warm-up. [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29421) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.29421v1)
- **SWE-ZERO to SWE-HERO** — Two-stage SFT recipe; releases 300k SWE-ZERO + 13k SWE-HERO trajectories distilled from Qwen3-Coder-480B; SWE-HERO-32B reaches 62.2% on SWE-bench. [HF Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=SWE-bench+Pro)
- **KLong: Open-Source LLM Agent for Extremely Long-Horizon Tasks** — Cold-start SFT + progressive RL; "Research-Factory" pipeline distilling Claude 4.5 Sonnet (Thinking). [HF Papers](https://huggingface.co/papers?q=SWE-bench+Pro)
- **SkillGym** — arXiv:2609.27717v2 (CC BY 4.0). Internalizes large-scale human skills; releases environments and trajectories. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27717v2)
- **Measuring Reproducibility in LLM Research** — ACM REP '26. 120-paper corpus with L1–L4 reproducibility rubric. [ACM](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3820002.3828584)

### Multimodal (VLMs, Video/Audio, Omni-Modal)
- **Training Object Permanence in World Models** — HF 2609.28654 (**198 upvotes — top paper**). Trains video world models for object permanence/solidity; new 3D + V2V benchmark. [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.28654)
- **Multimodal Thinking with Renderable Programs (SVGLM)** — arXiv:2609.30130. Uses SVG primitives as a medium for VLM "thinking" and active visual editing. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.30130v1)
- **Pushing the Frontiers of Universal Omni-Modal Embeddings** — arXiv:2609.25165. Builds on Qwen2.5-Omni Thinker–Talker for text/image/video/audio embeddings. [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.25165)
- **AV-GRPO** — HF 2609.29816. RL for joint audio-video generation with modality-anchored decoupling. [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.29816)
- **WanPE: Cinematic Prompt Enhancement for Text-to-Video** — HF 2609.30221 (33 upvotes). [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.30221)
- **VideoX-Qwen** — arXiv:2609.26015. Unified Qwen–Wan video editor; best mean result on 9/11 metrics vs. UniVideo and Kling O1. [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.26015)
- **OmniEcho: Spatial Audio Understanding for Embodied Agents** — HF 2609.23407 (20 upvotes). [HF](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.23407)
- **MultiVENT-Raw** — arXiv:2609.28437. Benchmark for retrieval/reasoning over raw phone/CCTV video. [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.28437)
- **What Looks Like a Capability Limit in Vision–Language Models** — arXiv:2609.27408. Shows apparent VLM coordinate-task limits are output-convention artifacts; rankings invert by convention. [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.27408v1)
- **CVP: Central-Peripheral Vision-Inspired Multimodal Model** — WACV 2026 (UC San Diego & Lambda). 3D spatial reasoning on LLaVA-Video-7B; beats Video-3D-LLM on ScanQA, SQA3D, ScanRefer, Multi3DRefer, Scan2Cap. [Lambda](https://lambda.ai/blog/cvp-spatial-reasoning)

## 4. Synthesis & Emerging Trends

1. **Cost-per-task is the new frontier metric.** The same-day OpenAI/Anthropic price cuts (Sep 22) signal that raw benchmark leadership is commoditizing; vendors now compete on *dollars per completed agentic task* (e.g., GPT-6 Sol at $0.27/task on AutomationBench). Expect continued downward pressure on inference pricing.

2. **Reasoning effort has become a first-class interface.** OpenAI, DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, and Mistral all expose controllable reasoning levels. Research is racing to match — test-time scaling papers (Planned TTS, inference-time capability/efficiency scaling) and the provocative "RL = sparse policy selection, not capability learning" position paper suggest the field is still debating *what* reasoning training actually buys.

3. **Open weights are consolidating around Chinese labs — with a licensing catch.** DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, and Kimi now dominate self-hostable frontier models, but the largest releases (Qwen3.8-Max, Kimi K3) are moving to custom, non-OSI licenses. The permissive Apache/MIT tier is increasingly reserved for smaller/mid-size models.

4. **Multimodal is converging on real-time, omni-modal interaction.** Lip-synced video avatars, full-duplex voice, and native-audio video generation all shipped in one week. Research mirrors this with omni-modal embeddings, joint audio-video RL (AV-GRPO), and spatial-audio embodied agents.

5. **World models and verifiers are the emerging research frontier.** The top-voted paper (object permanence in world models, 198 upvotes) and the surge of verifier/process-reward work (Agentic Verifier, verifiable process rewards) point to a shift from "bigger LLMs" toward *grounded, verifiable, environment-aware* systems.

6. **Reproducibility is becoming a first-class concern.** Rufus-Air's full 8-stage recipe, SWE-ZERO/SWE-HERO's 300k+ released trajectories, and the ACM REP reproducibility rubric all reflect growing demand for open, auditable training pipelines.
